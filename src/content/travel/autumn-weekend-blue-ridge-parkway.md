We loaded the van with cocoa, sketchbooks, and Psalm 19 ringing in our ears: *The heavens declare the glory of God.* The Parkway greeted us with layers of amber and blush, and every overlook felt like stepping inside a watercolor painting. We kept our itinerary simple—Moses H. Cone Memorial Park for an easy loop, lunch at a roadside picnic table, and a stop at Linville Falls so the kids could feel the spray on their cheeks.

In the evenings we stayed at a small family-run inn where quilts smelled like lavender. After dinner we journaled highlights and prayed for the people who maintain the trails we love. Traveling as a family isn't always tidy, but the goal is formation, not perfection. The mountains gave us room to breathe and reminded us that God's faithfulness stretches farther than the horizon.
