October is my cue to make space for the season ahead. Rather than emptying entire rooms, I focus on three touch points: entry table, coffee table, and kitchen counter. Each afternoon I set a timer for ten minutes, keep a donation bag nearby, and ask, *Do we still need this?* If not, it goes straight to the trunk. Small decisions add up to open space before we even think about garlands.

Once the surfaces breathe, I add back only what sparks gratitude—usually a Bible, a crock of wooden spoons, a single framed photo. The kids notice the difference immediately and seem calmer during homework hour. Making room for guests and sacred celebrations starts with honoring the space we already have. Gentle decluttering keeps hospitality feeling hopeful instead of hurried.
