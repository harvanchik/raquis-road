By 7:30 p.m., the living room looks like the day had opinions of its own. Instead of sighing, we start our evening reset. I turn on a soft lamp, cue Deborah Joy Winans on the speaker, and hand each child a woven basket. We call it a treasure hunt because words matter when you're shaping attitudes.

Everyone gathers their belongings while I reset pillows, spritz linen spray, and light a small rose geranium candle. When the baskets are empty, we huddle on the rug for a brief prayer of thanks and a blessing over rest. The entire ritual takes fifteen minutes, but it tells our home the day is finished. Calm doesn't require silence—it just needs a rhythm we repeat with love.
